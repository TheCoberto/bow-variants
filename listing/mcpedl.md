## Title
Bow Variants: Demolition Bow and Bow of Levitation

## Short description
Two craftable bows: one fires arrows that explode on impact, the other sends whatever it hits floating into the air.

## Introduction
Regular arrows getting boring? Bow Variants adds two new bows with their own arrows. The Demolition Bow's arrows blow up when they land, without breaking any blocks, and the Bow of Levitation sends mobs floating helplessly for 10 seconds. Both can be crafted in survival. No experiments needed.

## Description
- **Demolition Bow** + **Explosive Arrows**: arrows explode on impact. They don't break blocks, so they're safe to use near your base.
- **Bow of Levitation** + **Levitation Arrows**: whatever you hit floats for 10 seconds.

Both bows animate as you draw them, and the arrows leave a particle trail.

**Crafting** (crafting table)
- **Demolition Bow**: bow in the middle, 3 TNT down the left column, 3 string down the right.
- **Bow of Levitation**: bow in the middle, 3 blaze rods down the left column, 3 blaze powder down the right.
- **Explosive Arrow**: feather, arrow and TNT in a row.
- **Levitation Arrow**: feather, arrow and blaze powder in a row.

## Installation
1. Download the .mcaddon and open it. Minecraft imports both packs.
2. Create or edit a world and turn on the Bow Variants behavior pack (the resource pack is added with it). No experiments needed.
3. Don't use it in the same world as Skeleton Variants or Coberto's Arsenal; they already include these bows.
4. Upgrading from the old Explosive Arrow add-on? Remove its packs from your world first.

## Supported Minecraft versions
1.21.20 and newer
