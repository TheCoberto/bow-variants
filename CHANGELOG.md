- Explosive Arrow is now Bow Variants, rebuilt from scratch with new packs

- Bow of Levitation: fires levitation arrows that make whatever they hit float for 10 seconds
- Arrow trail particles now work
- New pack UUIDs: remove the old Explosive Arrow packs from your world before adding it
